What you get, what it costs, and what happens if it’s wrong.

Every service below is bought on its own — its own scope, price and guarantee. There is nothing to bundle and nothing to subscribe to.

How ordering works
  1. You send a brief. Each service page has a short brief to copy — three or four questions, no more. No account, no form service, no newsletter.
  2. You get a written quote — the exact scope, the fixed price, and a committed delivery date. If a service can’t be done well for your case, that reply says so instead of quoting it.
  3. Then a PayPal invoice. Payment runs through PayPal, so no card or bank details are handled by us or stored anywhere on this site.
  4. Work starts once the invoice is paid, and the finished work arrives by email on the committed date.
  5. Support is a reply to that delivery email. Same channel as the sale — no ticket system, no portal login.

Sending a brief is not a purchase and commits you to nothing. Nothing is charged before you have a scope and price in writing.

One round included with a Starter Guide, two with a Full Reference.

Lead Generation

You get
A list of businesses matching criteria you set — industry, geography, and what “qualified” means for you — built from public records, with the sources and research date stated in the delivery note.
Revisions
Criteria are agreed before the work starts. Records that do not match them are replaced or refunded rather than revised.
Delivery
A date is committed in the written quote. $25–50 covers a segment and geography already worked; a genuinely new segment is quoted once criteria are known, against the real research cost.
Guarantee
If delivered records materially do not match the agreed criteria — wrong city, wrong industry, entries that look fabricated — full refund.
Cancellation
A one-off deliverable, not a subscription — nothing to cancel.
Not covered
A guaranteed row count. If real qualifying businesses in your segment are scarce, you get the smaller list and you will know before you pay — a thin result is not grounds for refund. Public records only: nothing scraped from behind a login, and no contacting anyone on your behalf. Complying with the marketing and privacy rules that govern how you contact these businesses is your responsibility.

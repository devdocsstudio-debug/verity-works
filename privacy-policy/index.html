What we collect, what we don’t, and who else sees it.

Verity Works is a one-person professional services business operated by Noam Mor. This page describes what actually happens to data, checked against the live site rather than written from a template.

Last updated 19 October 2026

The short version

  • This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics. There is no tag manager, no advertising pixel and no social tracking script on any page. We verified this by loading every public page in a real browser and recording what it stored and who it contacted — not by reading our own source code.
  • There is no form, no account and no login. Every service is ordered by email, which means you decide what to send us and you can see it before you send it.
  • We never see your card or bank details. Payment runs through PayPal invoices.
  • We do hold business contact details for sales outreach, and we will delete yours on request. That section is below, in plain terms, because it is the part most privacy policies bury.

This website

Cookies
None. No page on this site sets a cookie, and nothing here asks for cookie consent because there is nothing to consent to.
Analytics
None running. No visitor analytics, heatmaps, session recording or A/B testing. If we ever add analytics, this page changes before it goes live, and we will say which tool it is.
Browser storage
None. Nothing is written to local storage, session storage or IndexedDB on any page.
Scripts on this site
Three small scripts, all first-party, none of which send anything anywhere. One opens and closes the navigation menu. One copies an order brief to your clipboard when you press the copy button. One reads a ?ref= label from the page address, if a link you followed had one, and adds it as a line of text inside the email draft — so a reply tells us which channel it came from. That line is ordinary visible text in the draft: you can read it, edit it or delete it before sending, and nothing is recorded unless you send the email.
Fonts
Typefaces are served by Google Fonts, so your browser requests files from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. As with any request your browser makes, Google receives your IP address and browser user-agent in order to answer it. We do not control and cannot see that; what Google does with it is governed by Google’s privacy policy.
Hosting
The site is static files served by GitHub Pages. GitHub operates the servers and, as host, processes connection information such as IP addresses in its own logs. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not receive, and have no access to, those server logs.
Search Console
We use Google Search Console to see how this site performs in search. It reports aggregate counts and search queries for the site as a whole. It does not identify individual visitors to us.

When you email us

What you send
Whatever you choose to put in the email. Each service page offers a short brief — three or four questions such as your business name, the platforms you care about, your audience, the source material for a repurposing job, or anything you do not want quoted publicly. You are not obliged to answer any of them, and you can see exactly what the draft contains before you send it.
Where it goes
To devdocsstudio@gmail.com, a Gmail mailbox, which means Google processes and stores that mail as our email provider. If you would rather not send something through Gmail, say so and we will agree another route.
Automated inbox checks
We run our own tooling against that mailbox, with read-only access, to notice replies promptly. It reads our own inbox; it does not reply, negotiate, invoice or take any action on its own, and it sends nothing to third parties.
Source material you give us
Used only to produce your deliverable. We do not publish it, quote it elsewhere, sell it, or use it to train any model. If you tell us something in the source must not be quoted publicly, that instruction is part of the job and is checked before delivery.
What we never ask for
Passwords, card numbers, bank details, government identifiers, or access to your accounts. No Verity Works service requires any of these. If a message appearing to come from us asks for one, it is not from us.

Payment

How it works
We send a PayPal invoice and you pay PayPal directly. Card and bank details never reach us and are not stored anywhere on this site.
What we keep
The invoice record itself — who was invoiced, for what service, the amount, and whether it was paid — because that is our business and tax record. PayPal’s own handling of your payment is governed by their privacy policy, not ours.

Business contact details we hold for outreach

If you received an email from us that you did not ask for, this is the section that applies to you.

What we hold
For a small number of organisations we have researched as potential clients: company name, a publicly listed business contact address, sometimes a publicly listed business phone number or the name and role of the person publicly identified as the relevant contact, plus our notes on why we thought the service might fit and the public page we read it from.
Where it came from
Public sources only — an organisation’s own website, its public documentation, its public issue tracker, or its published podcast or article feed. We do not buy contact lists, we do not guess email addresses, and we do not scrape social networks for profiles.
Why
To decide whether it is worth contacting an organisation about a service, and to write to them about it. Our commercial email identifies who it is from and tells you how to stop receiving it.
Opting out
Reply to the email, or write to devdocsstudio@gmail.com with “do not contact me”. We add you to a suppression list so you are not contacted again. We keep the minimum needed to honour that — keeping the address is what makes it possible not to write to it again. If you would rather be erased entirely instead, say so and we will do that.

The Lead Generation service and public records

What the data is
Business registration records published by the State of Colorado as open data. Every list we deliver carries the attribution and disclaimer the State requires, and names the date the records were pulled.
Addresses
Delivered lists give street, city and zip, with house numbers withheld by default, because most newly formed companies register at the owner’s home address. Full addresses are provided only on a buyer’s explicit request, and the delivery note tells the buyer that using the data lawfully is their responsibility.
If a record is about you
We cannot change a public record held by the State — that has to go to the State. Write to us and we will remove you from any list we still hold and will not include you in future ones.

The Verity Works LinkedIn application

We operate a LinkedIn application used solely to publish Verity Works’ own posts to its operator’s own LinkedIn account.

Whose account
The operator’s own. The application is not offered to other people, has no other users, and no one else signs in to it.
What it accesses
It requests only openid and profile, to confirm which account it is signed in as, and w_member_social, to publish a post as that account.
What it does not do
It does not read your LinkedIn profile, your connections, your feed, your messages or your activity. It does not collect, store or process personal data about any LinkedIn member other than the account holder. It does not send connection invitations, messages or InMail, and it has no access to any other member’s data.
What it stores
The text of the posts we wrote ourselves, and an access token for the operator’s own account. Nothing about anyone else.
Finding people to connect with
Where we identify organisations worth approaching, candidates come from ordinary public sources and the result is a link to a LinkedIn search page that the operator opens manually. No profile is harvested, stored or automated against, and any invitation is sent by a person, by hand.

Keeping, sharing and your choices

How long we keep things
Client project material is kept while we may still owe you revisions or support under the service terms, and invoice records are kept as long as business and tax records require. Prospect research notes are deleted once an organisation is no longer a realistic client. Suppression entries are kept indefinitely on purpose, because their whole function is to prevent future contact.
Who else receives data
Only the service providers named on this page, each doing the job it is named for: Google (email, fonts, search reporting), GitHub (hosting), PayPal (payment), LinkedIn (publishing our own posts). We never sell or rent anything you tell us, we do not share it for advertising, and we do not use it to train AI models. Our Lead Generation service does sell lists compiled from public business-registration records, which is a different thing and is described in its own section above — it never contains client or website-visitor data.
AI in how we work
We use AI tooling to help produce deliverables, and a person reviews every deliverable before it is sent. Your material is used to do your job, not to improve a model for anyone else.
Requests
Write to devdocsstudio@gmail.com and ask what we hold about you, ask for it corrected, ask for it deleted, or ask us never to contact you again. We will answer, and we will do it. We may need to confirm you are who you say you are before handing anything over, and we may keep what the law requires us to keep — if so we will tell you what and why.
Children
These are business-to-business services. They are not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information about anyone under 16.
Security
This site is served over HTTPS and holds no personal data of its own, because it has no database, no form handler and no accounts — there is nothing here to breach. Access credentials for the services above are held only on the operator’s own computer, never on this website or any server we run. No system is beyond compromise and we make no security guarantees; if a breach ever affected your data we would tell you what happened.
Changes
If what we do changes, this page is updated and the date at the top changes with it. Material changes to how we handle data already given to us will be told to affected clients directly, not just posted here.
Contact
Verity Works, operated by Noam Mor — devdocsstudio@gmail.com. One person reads that mailbox, so a privacy request goes straight to the person who can act on it.

This page describes our actual practices in plain language. It is not legal advice, and it does not assert certification or a finding of compliance under any particular privacy law. If a specific legal framework applies to you and you need to know how we handle a requirement under it, ask us and we will answer honestly, including where the answer is that we do not yet know.
